Size Guide

Find Your Size

Piercing Ring Size Guides

Here are the key measurements you’ll need to know for piercing rings:

  • Gauge - the thickness of the ring, can be measured in (g) or (mm). For ease, we use (mm).
  • Diameter - the internal diameter; measured from the inner edges of the ring.

When choosing a size (e.g. 1.2 x 8mm), the first number refers to gauge and the second refers to diameter.

If you have a new piercing, we recommend a ring with a larger internal diameter, to allow room for potential swelling. Once the piercing has fully healed you could look at choosing a ring with a smaller diameter.

Please check the size that you require for your piercing and ensure that you add the size that you need to your cart. This cannot be changed after checkout.

To find out more about sizes and measurements, read our guide .

Piercing Bar Size Guides

Here are the key measurements you’ll need to know for piercing bars:

  • Gauge - the thickness of the bar, can be measured in (g) or (mm). For ease, we use (mm).
  • Length - the wearable length of the bar; measured between the balls, not including them.

We always pierce with specific sized jewellery to help promote comfort and healing. Find this information on your Blue Banana booking confirmation. Alternatively, use our What’s My Gauge helper here, or Book a Consultation with one of our expert piercers for sizing help.

To find out more about sizes and measurements, read our guide .

Established in 1997, Blue Banana is the number one place for all things alternative in the UK. Since the beginning, we’ve specialised in body piercing and modification with over one million piercings performed in the past 25 years in our high street stores. If you’re looking to get your next piercing then use our store locator to find your local Blue Banana scattered across England & Wales. If you’ve been searching for body piercing near me then Blue Banana is the place for you!

Blue Banana isn’t just an online presence, we are always expanding and have stores throughout the country including the South West, Midlands and Wales. Equipped with the latest fashion, footwear and accessories as well as our professional piercing studio, we are a haven for any gothic or alternative individual.

Each of our stores is complete with a professional, sterilised studio ready to perform all our piercings from ear and nose piercings to navel and surface piercings. With fully trained staff and the latest piercing techniques, you can rest assured that your Blue Banana piercing is in expert hands. Our staff undergo months of training to become licensed piercers and will always make sure that you are comfortable and fully informed every step of the way. All piercings at Blue Banana are carried out with needles so you won’t find any piercing guns in our studios. Using a piercing needle is a much cleaner and more precise method than using a piercing gun, therefore, causing less trauma to the piercing site and allowing for fast healing. We prioritise healing here at Blue Banana so we make sure to provide top-notch aftercare knowledge too. The stores are on hand to offer advice and information via our social media platforms. You can reach out to our stores via Facebook, Instagram or email to speak to a member of the piercing team with your piercing or aftercare queries. They’re always happy to help!

Do you have to book piercings at Blue Banana?

At Blue Banana, we offer a walk-in service so there is no need to book! We do not have a booking system for piercing appointments as it is on a first-come, first-serve basis. When you visit the store, a member of the team will book you in. If it is busy then you may be booked in and asked to come back later via our text-back system. Our helpful team will discuss the piercing process with you when you get booked in and can assist you with any questions you may have.

If you’re looking for Blue Banana piercing prices then be sure to view our pricing tables for each store. You can also find our Blue Banana piercing age restrictions here. It is important to check if you are of the correct age prior to booking your piercing to avoid disappointment. Our team will likely ask for a valid photo ID if you do not look of the appropriate age for the piercing.

How many piercings can you get at once at Blue Banana?

Our Blue Banana team is happy to perform as many piercings as you can handle at once although three to four piercings are the recommended amount for one sitting. Our piercers may suggest otherwise if they feel that your healing will be compromised. Our team will always prioritise your safety and healing when it comes to getting your piercings.

Do Blue Banana change piercings for you?

When your piercing has fully healed, we’ll be happy to change your piercing jewellery to something new. Please note that this will incur a £10 charge for body and microdermal jewellery changes. We also offer genital piercing changes and microdermal removal for £20.
