How it works

  1. Join the circle

    Free, in any store or online, in about a minute. You'll get 10 % off your first order or a free jewellery upgrade for turning up.

  2. Gather as you go

    Two points for every £1, whether you're buying a dress, a dye, a pair of boots or a new piece of jewellery. One account at the till, in the app and on the web.

  3. Claim your due

    100 points becomes a £2 reward to spend in store or online. Or pass it on — points can be given as a donation instead.

In the studio

Ten per cent off every piercing, jewellery included. It holds in all of our studios, on your first piercing and your fifth, with no voucher to remember and nothing to book in advance.

A fresh piercing is looked after, too. We check in at day 3, 14, 30 and 60 to see how it's healing, and there's an offer waiting on your first jewellery change once it has.

Get pierced together and you both earn: referrals are rewarded for the pair of you, not just the one who booked.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. The Coven is free to join and free to stay in, in any of our 18 UK stores or online.

How do points work?

You earn 2 points for every £1 you spend, in store or online. 100 points becomes a £2 reward you can spend either way. One account, one balance, wherever you shop.

Are there tiers or levels?

No. Everyone in the Coven is in the same circle with the same rules — there's no status to climb and nothing to maintain.

Do I need to carry a card?

Show your card at the till for in-store offers, or sign in online. Your account is recognised at the till, in the app and on the website.

Is jewellery included?

Yes. Members get 10 % off all piercings in our studios, jewellery included.
