The small print

Questions

Is it actually free?

Yes. Joining the Inner Circle costs nothing, in any of our 18 UK stores or online.

How many points do I get?

2 points for every £1 you spend. 100 points turns into a £2 reward you can use in store or online.

Are there tiers?

No. Everyone in the Circle gets the same programme and the same rules — nothing to climb, nothing to keep up.

Does it work online and in store?

Both. One account is recognised at the till, in the app and on the website, so your points land in the same place wherever you shop.

Is jewellery included in the piercing discount?

It is — members get 10 % off all piercings in our studios, jewellery included.
