How it works

  1. Get your card

    Sign up free at any till or online. You'll start with 10 % off your first order, or a free jewellery upgrade — your pick.

  2. Earn on everything

    2 points per £1 across all 18 stores and the website. Clothing, band merch, boots, dye, jewellery — it all counts.

  3. Cash them in

    100 points = £2 off, in store or online. Or turn your points into a donation instead.

The rest of it

  • First look at drops

    24 to 48 hours ahead of everyone else on new collections and collabs.

  • Aftercare that follows up

    Check-ins at day 3, 14, 30 and 60 after a piercing, plus an offer on your first jewellery change.

  • Bring someone in

    Get pierced together and you both get rewarded for the referral.

  • Points for showing up

    Reviews, tagged photos and referrals earn points too, not just spending.

  • Give them away

    Swap your points for a charity donation instead of a voucher.

Questions

Is it actually free?

Yes. Joining the Inner Circle costs nothing, in any of our 18 UK stores or online.

How many points do I get?

2 points for every £1 you spend. 100 points turns into a £2 reward you can use in store or online.

Are there tiers?

No. Everyone in the Inner Circle gets the same programme and the same rules — nothing to climb, nothing to keep up.

Online and in store?

Both. One account is recognised at the till, in the app and on the website, so your points land in the same place wherever you shop.

Is jewellery included?

It is — members get 10 % off all piercings in our studios, jewellery included.
