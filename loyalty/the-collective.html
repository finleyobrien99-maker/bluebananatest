FAQs

Good to know

What does it cost?

Nothing — the Collective is free to join and free to stay in, in any of our 18 UK stores or online.

How do points work?

2 points for every £1 spent, in store or online. 100 points becomes a £2 reward you can use either way.

Are there tiers?

No. Every member is on the same programme with the same rules — there's nothing to climb.

Does everything count?

Yes. Clothing, band merch, footwear, accessories, hair dye, piercing jewellery and piercings themselves all earn points.

Do I need the card with me?

Show it at the till for in-store offers, or sign in online. One account is recognised at the till, in the app and on the web.

Can I donate my points?

You can — swap your points for a charity donation instead of a voucher whenever you like.
