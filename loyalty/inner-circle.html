FAQs

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Inner Circle is free to join and free to stay in, in any of our 18 UK stores.

How do I join?

Ask at the till in any of our 18 UK stores. We'll help you save your Inner Circle card to your phone using Wallet Crew.

What do I get when I join?

50 bonus points as soon as you sign up, plus a welcome reward of 10% off or a free jewellery upgrade.

How do points work?

You get 1 point for every £1 you spend in our stores. Every 100 points is worth £2 off your next in-store purchase.

How do I check my points?

Your points balance shows on your Inner Circle card on your phone, so you can check it any time.

Can I use Inner Circle online?

Not at the moment. Inner Circle is an in-store scheme for now, so you collect and spend your points in our stores.

Does everything count?

Yes. Clothing, band merch, footwear, accessories, hair dye, piercing jewellery and piercings bought in store all earn points.

How do I get 10% off my piercing?

Show your Inner Circle card at the studio before you're pierced. The 10% comes off the piercing and the jewellery that goes in.

Where can I use the exclusive offers?

Member offers are redeemable in store only. Show your card at the till to use them.

Are there tiers or levels?

No. Every member gets the same benefits from the day they join.
